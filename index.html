Our story

Frances built it. Anna is carrying it on.

Founder Frances is retiring after decades of running Manna. Her daughter, Anna Drennen, is taking over the store.

The shop stays independent and stays local. Many Tuscaloosa families have shopped here for years, and the doors remain open to them and to anyone new.
